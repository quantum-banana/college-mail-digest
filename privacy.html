Privacy Policy

College Mail Digest Metadata

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Operator and purpose

This application is maintained by quantum-banana exclusively for personal college email review. It helps its owner identify relevant notices and prepare summaries, actions, deadlines and source links. It is not distributed as a public email service.

Information accessed

With the owner's Google authorization, the assistant uses gmail.readonly to access the approved account's identity, message IDs, headers, dates, labels and selected inline message text. The scope permits reading the mailbox; individual runs are limited by the owner's instructions. The helper does not request Gmail write permissions or retrieve attachment endpoints.

How information is used

Email information supports the owner's requested summaries. Minimal message identifiers and processing records prevent duplicate reporting and allow recovery after interrupted runs. The application does not sell email information, use it for advertising, or itself train general-purpose AI models.

Processing through OpenAI

When the owner requests a digest in Codex, selected email information is processed by OpenAI/Codex to generate that response. OpenAI's handling of chat content is governed by the owner's service, account settings and applicable OpenAI policies. This policy makes no separate claim that OpenAI retains no chat data or that the owner's account data controls have been independently audited.

Local storage and protection

The reusable Google refresh credential is protected locally by Windows CurrentUser DPAPI and private file permissions. Access tokens are kept in memory. The assistant's helpers do not save complete message bodies, raw MIME or attachments locally. Local processing state and prepared reports contain minimal identifiers, source metadata, links and summaries. Codex chat records are separate from these local files and follow the service's retention rules.

Retention and removal

Local state and reports remain until the owner chooses to remove them; the application does not currently apply an automatic retention period. Removing local reports does not remove Codex chat history. The owner manages those records through the relevant service controls.

Control and withdrawal

The owner may stop using the assistant and revoke the application's access in their Google Account. Deleting a local credential file alone does not revoke Google's authorization. The assistant does not automatically reauthorize after access is revoked or expires. Google Workspace administrator policies may also restrict access.

This public website

GitHub Pages hosts these informational pages. The website has no Gmail connection, login form, analytics scripts or tracking cookies added by this application, and does not publish email contents or credentials. GitHub may process technical access information when serving the site under its own privacy policies.
