College Mail Digest Metadata

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Personal use

College Mail Digest Metadata is maintained by quantum-banana for personal use with one approved college mailbox. It is not a public email service or an official VIT service. Its account guard does not support other mailboxes.

Authorization and control

The owner controls Google consent and each requested run. Gmail access uses gmail.readonly. The Gmail helper does not perform email-writing actions. Granting Gmail authorization does not itself create a schedule or authorize additional tasks.

Review original information

Email summaries can omit context or contain errors. Review the original message and official college information before relying on a deadline, instruction or important decision. Attachments are not examined by the read-only connection helper.

Access and availability

Access depends on Google authorization, Workspace policy and the local runtime. Production publishing is not a promise that authorization or execution will remain available indefinitely.

Stopping use

The owner may stop using the application and revoke its Google access at any time. Data handling, local retention and OpenAI processing are described in the Privacy Policy.
